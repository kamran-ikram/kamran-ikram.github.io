Cover story

World Electric Vehicle Journal, Volume 17, Issue 8, August 2026

A review of electric vehicle integration in peer-to-peer energy networks

Electric cars are usually seen as a problem for the grid. This review looks at how they can help instead: charging and discharging at the right times, and trading energy with neighbours. It also points out that most of the benefits reported so far come from simulations, not real streets.
